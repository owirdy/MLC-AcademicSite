## Abstract

GaussianStream introduces a novel framework for real-time 3D scene reconstruction from monocular video using an efficient Gaussian splatting representation. Our method achieves state-of-the-art reconstruction quality at interactive frame rates, enabling downstream applications in AR/VR and robotics.
